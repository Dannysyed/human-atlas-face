head.glb — Human Atlas 3D head & face prototype model

Derived from the Z-Anatomy 3D models (https://www.z-anatomy.com/, FBX files from
https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy, Resources/Models/FBX), which are themselves based on
BodyParts3D (c) The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS).

Changes made: selected head/neck structures only; world transforms applied; skin regions
subdivided; meshes decimated; left/right parts kept separate; some parts merged (teeth, eyeball
coats, auricle sub-regions); stable anatomical IDs stored in glTF node extras; Draco compression.
